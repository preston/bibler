## Bible Study Group Leader: Example Inputs

### Life of Mary

Goal: Understand the major life of events of Jesus' mother, Mary, her role in Jesus' life, and the challenges she had to overcome.
Instruction: Create a 1-hour lesson covering the role and life of Jesus' mother, Mary, and notably events in her life as covered by the New Testament. Add a 5 minute break in the middle. I'd like a 15 minute discussion at the end centering on key challenges Mary may have faced, and what she could do in her faith to overcome them.

### Christian Rituals: Scripture and Dogma

Goal: Identify truths and myths of comman Christian practices in popular culture.
Instruction: We're doing a study/discussion on myths and truths of non-scriptural habits of modern Christians, such as certain ritualistic practices performed by Catholics. Create a service plan with 45-minute sermon covering or any origins and myths surrounding these, opening with 3 worship songs and closing with prayer and a final song.

Instruction: I need a youth service about overcoming temptation and do the right thing under peer pressure. This is for middle school and high school-aged children. Please include about 30 minutes of spoken sermon material for the sermon portion across 3 sections.

### Christian Kids

Goal: Learn how to follow God's instructions to honor your parents
Instruction: I'm planning an elementary school-aged service for younger kids on honoring and respecting your parents. I'd like 3-4 sing-a-long worship songs, then have two 15-minute fun sermons with fun group activities after each one. The service should open and close with prayer, and the last thing should be a song.
